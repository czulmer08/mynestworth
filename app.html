Spreadsheet & maintenance
Show each itemized category's bills as indented lines under it in your Account Summary tab (with their own budget & spent). Kept up to date automatically after you edit bills.
Tidies your transactions to the top, deletes the leftover empty rows (reclaiming space so imports don’t hit Google’s size limit), cleans the Year/Month columns to plain numbers (imports sometimes leave dates there), and rewrites the summary formulas to whole-column so they’re fast and never tied to a row count. Safe — no transactions or budget are removed.
Saves a dated copy of your whole workbook to your Google Drive, so you always have a restore point.
‘Erase’ clears your logged transactions, goals, and net-worth history but keeps your categories, budgets, and income set up. This can’t be undone.
